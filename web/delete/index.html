Delete your Fog Messenger account

You can delete your account in a browser, including after uninstalling Fog. Delete my account online.

How to delete from your main device

  1. Open Fog on your main device (the one with your recovery phrase).
  2. Tap the menu button (☰) at the top left to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete account.

Deletion is permanent once the server confirms it. A browser with your main-device key can use it directly. In a fresh browser, your recovery phrase proves ownership locally. The words never go to the server or support. If you need help, email privacy@getfog.org.

What Fog removes from the active service

  • Your account ID and public keys
  • Your username (anyone can claim it afterwards)
  • Linked device registrations and their access to the account
  • Messages still waiting to be delivered to you
  • Push notification tokens
  • Your encrypted chat backup
  • Abuse reports you filed
  • Fog’s local account data in the app or browser session used for deletion. On other devices, clear the app’s local data to remove any remaining copies.

What is kept

  • Abuse reports other people filed about your account (just the two account IDs and a time), kept for abuse prevention.
  • Encrypted attachments you sent and encrypted history archives uploaded when linking devices are eligible for hourly cleanup 45 days after upload.
  • Messages you already sent can remain on recipients’ devices and in their backups. Encrypted copies still queued for other devices remain until acknowledged or cleaned up after 30 days. Ordinary delivery records can include your sender ID during that time.
